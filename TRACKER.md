- **2026-08-29** — Security audit of the app itself: **no exploitable vulnerabilities**. Three concrete attack hypotheses were formed and all three disproved by testing rather than reading. URL scheme injection is unreachable because `localURLString` interpolates only a `UInt16` into a literal scheme.
